## Design Context

Audience: industrial buyers and engineers evaluating refractory materials, in English and Arabic (RTL). Brand: Ajyad Thermotech (dragon-and-torch logo, blue with an orange flame accent).

Established, precise, dependable, with energy. Ajyad blue, flame orange accents, warm mineral neutrals, charcoal, strong condensed headings, manufacturing photography, generous space. Motion is expressive but purposeful (requested: "lots of animation"): orchestrated hero entrance, staggered scroll reveals, directional micro-interactions that follow reading direction, fast feedback. Ease-out curves only, transform/opacity only. Responsive pages, clear product and enquiry paths, keyboard access, and full prefers-reduced-motion support.
