## Design Context

Audience: industrial buyers and engineers evaluating refractory materials. The user supplied asfourmr.com and selected a refreshed design using Asfour branding and content.

Established, precise, dependable. Asfour blue, warm mineral neutrals, charcoal, strong condensed headings, real manufacturing photographs, generous space, and restrained motion. Responsive pages, clear product and enquiry paths, keyboard access, and reduced-motion support.
